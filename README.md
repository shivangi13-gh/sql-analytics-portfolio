# Projects

## 1. Music Store Data Analysis

**Description:**  
This project analyzes a music store dataset using SQL to uncover insights related to sales, customer behavior, and music trends. The analysis covers multiple business questions ranging from customer spending to genre popularity.

**Key Insights:**  
- The city generating the highest revenue is Prague with total sales of $273.24
- The highest spending customer is R Madhav, with total spending of $144.54
- The country with the most invoices is USA with 131 invoices 
- Rock is one of the most popular genres, with top artist Led Zeppelin contributing 114 tracks
- The most popular genre varies by country, with Alternative & Punk leading in Argentina
- Genre preferences vary by country, highlighting regional differences in music trends

**Conclusion:**  
This project strengthened SQL skills through practical data analysis, including joins, CTEs, subqueries, and window functions. It demonstrates the ability to analyze relational data and extract meaningful business insights.
